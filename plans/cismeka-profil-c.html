    Process steps

    1. Laser cutting of the flat blank

      Blank 713 × 360,27 with the two end notches, the relief slits between tabs and upper part, and the 4 holes Ø9 (2 per tab). The CISMEKA text is laser engraved / marked, not cut through (closed letters would drop out).

    2. Deburring

      Break the edges, mainly in the notches and around the Ø9 holes. Check flatness before bending.

    3. Press brake: 6 bends

      Order: 2 tabs (part flat) → flip → 2 returns → 2 flanges. Back gauge and checks after each bend in the table below.

    4. Inspection

      Overall height 230, flanges 40, returns 35, tabs 30, all 6 bends square at 90°, Ø9 hole pitch and position.

    To confirm before production

    • Thickness and material: not on the photo. A 4,93 bend deduction at R3 fits 2,5 mm (K ≈ 0,35) or 3 mm (K = 0,5).
    • If your press brake deduction is different, redo the flat blank, otherwise 35 / 40 / 230 will not come out.
    • Dimensions scaled from the photo (not dimensioned): lower part ≈ 607, tabs ≈ 130 high, hole pitch ≈ 70, upper part offset ≈ 53 each side.
    • The 3D model uses sharp corners; real bends are R3.
