    Process steps

    1. Laser cutting of the flat blank

      E24 (S235) sheet, t = 2. Blank 1444,85 × 503,42 with the 2 end plates, the notch in the base on the right, corner reliefs and end plate holes.

    2. Deburring

      Break sharp edges (required in the title block), mainly in the notches and at the end plate corners.

    3. Press brake: 6 bends, all UP 90° R2

      Order: 2 small end plate returns → return 20 → base 189 → 2 end plates. V12 die (inside R ≈ 2 at t = 2). The last two bends need a tall punch (≥ 200 mm) because the 189 base stands up next to the tooling.

    4. Inspection, then painting

      1023 × 301 × 189, return 20, end plate returns 28,5, squareness of all 6 bends, hole positions. Paint after inspection.

    To confirm before production

    • Bends 5 and 6 need a punch ≥ 200 mm tall. With a standard punch, the base (189) or the end plate return hits the upper beam. Without that punch, weld separate end plates instead.
    • End plate hole diameter not readable. Position scaled: ≈ 100 from the base, ≈ 40 from the front edge.
    • Notch in the base (one side only): ≈ 135 deep × ≈ 100 long, scaled.
    • The end plate returns do not run the full height: they start ≈ 96 from the base (scaled).
    • The iso view may show holes in one end plate return: check on the paper drawing.
    • The 3D model uses sharp corners; real bends are R2.
