    Process steps

    1. Laser cutting of the flat blank

      E24 (S235) sheet, t = 3. Blank 894 × 162,63 (to confirm, see above) with the 2 notches ≈ 100 wide and ≈ 93 deep from the return edge, rounded corners, and the half-round R ≈ 20 on the bottom flange edge. 4 parts.

    2. Deburring

      Break sharp edges (required in the title block), mainly in the notches and the half-round.

    3. Press brake: 3 bends, all UP 90°

      Order: return 20 → top flange 30 → bottom flange 50. The part keeps the same face up. V24 die: R5 would need a V30, but the 20 return is too short for a V30 (minimum flange ≈ 21).

    4. Inspection, then painting

      80 / 50 / 30 / 20, 25 under the notches, squareness and straightness over 894. Paint after inspection.
