    Process steps

    1. Laser cutting of the flat blank

      256 × 175, t = 3, from the customer DXF. 2 windows 70 × 35 R5 in the flange (start ≈ 44,6 from the lip edge, 10 from each side, 15 apart). 8 Ø3,3 pilot holes for M4 in the leg: rows 6 and 155,5 from the leg end.

    2. Deburring

      Windows, holes and edges.

    3. Press brake: 2 bends

      Lip 90° first, then the 60° bend (120° inside). Same direction for both. V24 die (inside R ≈ 4). Check 120° with a template on the first part.

    4. Tapping 8 × M4

      After bending: the top row is right at the edge of the 120° bend zone and can go oval.

    5. Inspection

      Lip 40, leg 161,54, angles 90° and 120°, front view height 192,8, M4 positions ±0,1.
