    Process steps

    1. Laser cutting of the flat blank

      212,2 × 205 in S235JR t = 3 from the DXF: 2 × Ø5,5 in the foot, slot + round hole in the vertical part, 4 pilot holes Ø4,2 for M5 in the slanted part. 3 parts.

    2. Deburring

      Break the sharp corners (note on the drawing).

    3. Press brake: 2 bends, same direction

      Foot 90° first, then the 45° bend (135° inside). V24 die. Check 135° with a template on the first part.

    4. Tapping 4 × M5

      In the slanted part, rows 6 and 110,5 from the tip. ±0,1 on position.

    5. Inspection, then painting RAL

      Vertical straight 57,51, slanted straight 117,51, angle 135°, foot square, holes 28 from the back of the vertical part.
