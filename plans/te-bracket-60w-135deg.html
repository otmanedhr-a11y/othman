    Process steps

    1. Laser cutting of the flat blank

      202 × 60, t = 3, from the DXF: window and small holes in the flange, 2 pilot holes Ø3,3 for M4 in the slanted part (10 and 80 from the tip, on the centre line). 5 parts.

    2. Deburring

      Chamfer 0,5 × 45° on the sharp corners (note on the drawing).

    3. Press brake: 2 bends, same direction

      Lip 90° first, then the 45° bend (135° inside). V24 die. The part is only 60 wide: one gauge finger in the middle, short punch segment. Check 135° with a template on the first part.

    4. Tapping 2 × M4

      In the slanted part, ±0,1 on position.

    5. Inspection

      Lip 40, flange straight 57,51, slanted straight 107,51, angles 90° / 135°, M4 at 10 and 80 from the tip.
